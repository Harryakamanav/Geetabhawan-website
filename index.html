पवित्र परम्परा एवं सेवा • SACRED HERITAGE

A Sanctuary of Faith, Culture, and Selfless Service

Geeta Bhawan is a non-profit religious and social institution established under the aegis of Kirti Nagar Shree Sanatan Dharam Sabha (Regd.), serving Delhi with unwavering spiritual dedication.

More than 40 years old, Geeta Bhawan has been devotedly working towards keeping alive the vibrant spirit of Sanatan culture in our society. Dedicated to celebrating religion, culture, love, and faith towards our omnipresent God, our temple works untiringly not only on the religious front, but also for cultural and social welfare—regularly organizing community medical camps and humanitarian initiatives.

पावन मंदिर दर्शन • The Sacred Sanctums

Geeta Bhawan houses eight holy sanctum sanctorums consecrated under ancient Vedic traditions:

१
श्री लक्ष्मी नारायण मंदिर
Shri Laxmi Narayan Mandir

The principal sanctum dedicated to Bhagwan Vishnu, Devi Lakshmi, Radha-Krishna, and Ram Darbar in celestial golden shringar.

२
शिवालय
Shivalaya & Shivalingam

The sacred sanctum of Mahadev Shiva, Mata Parvati, Lord Ganesha, and holy Shivalingam for daily Jalabhishek.

३
पारदेश्वर शिवलिंग
Pardeshwar Ling (Mercury Shivalinga)

The rare and revered mercury (Paarad) Shivalinga consecrated in a Himalayan cave sanctum guarded by Nagraj.

४
माँ दुर्गा एवं श्री संकटमोचन हनुमान मंदिर
Mata and Hanuman Mandir

Opulent gold-vaulted ceiling sanctum enshrining Sherawali Mata Durga and Veer Hanuman Ji.

५
माँ सरस्वती मंदिर
Saraswati Mata Mandir

Sanctum of Devi Saraswati, Goddess of wisdom, eloquence, arts, and music, holding the sacred Veena.

६
श्री साईं बाबा मंदिर
Shri Sai Baba Mandir

Sanctum of Shri Sai Baba of Shirdi, radiating the eternal teachings of "श्रद्धा" (Faith) and "सबूरी" (Patience).

७
माँ शीतला मंदिर
Sheetla Mata Mandir

Revered sanctum dedicated to Mata Sheetla Ji, worshipped for health, maternal protection, and peace.

८
भगवान विष्णु के दशावतार
Vishnu Dashavatar Sanctum

Divine murti holding the Sudarshana Chakra aloft, honoring the ten cosmic incarnations of Bhagwan Vishnu.
