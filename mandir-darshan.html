Principal Sanctum

श्री लक्ष्मी नारायण मंदिर

SHRI LAXMI NARAYAN MANDIR

The central sanctum sanctorum of Geeta Bhawan enshrines Bhagwan Shri Vishnu and Devi Mahalakshmi, flanked by Shri Radha-Krishna and Shri Ram Darbar in exquisite white marble under carved canopies. Adorned in radiant golden pitambar vastra and peacock-feathered mukuts.

🕉️ Deities: Bhagwan Vishnu, Devi Lakshmi, Radha-Krishna, Ram Darbar
🪔 Key Rituals: Daily Shringar, Rajbhog, and Maha Sandhya Aarti

Mahadev Sanctum

शिवालय — शिव परिवार एवं शिवलिंग

SHIVALAYA & SHIVALINGAM

The holy Shivalaya is home to Mahadev Shiva, Mata Parvati, and Shri Ganesh Ji adorned in celestial vastra, as well as the consecrated white marble Jalabhishek Kund enshrining the dark stone Shivalingam with Nandi Maharaj and sacred Vedic mantra inscriptions.

🕉️ Deities: Lord Shiva, Mata Parvati, Lord Ganesha, Nandi Ji
🌿 Key Rituals: Daily Jalabhishek, Bilva Patra Archana, Maha Shivratri 4-Pahar Puja

Rare Mercury Lingam

पारदेश्वर शिवलिंग

PARDESHWAR LING SANCTUM

Consecrated inside an enchanting Himalayan cave-like sanctum illuminated in celestial azure light, the rare mercury (Paarad) Shivalinga is guarded by the divine multi-headed Nagraj, wooden bridge, and floral offerings. Worshipped for supreme spiritual purification.

🕉️ Significance: Consecrated mercury (Paarad) Shivalinga with Nagraj
🙏 Tradition: Pavitra Darshan & Meditative Dhyana

Shakti & Bhakti Sanctum

माँ दुर्गा एवं श्री हनुमान मंदिर

MATA & HANUMAN MANDIR

Set beneath an opulent, carved golden ceiling with an auspicious brass temple bell, this divine sanctum houses Sherawali Mata Durga in celestial white-and-silver vastra, and Sankatmochan Veer Hanuman Ji holding the Sanjeevani mountain in radiant shringar.

🕉️ Deities: Maa Durga, Shri Hanuman Ji
📖 Key Rituals: Weekly Tuesday Sundarkand Path (6:00 PM) & Navratri Pujan

Vidya & Sangeet Sanctum

माँ सरस्वती मंदिर

SARASWATI MATA MANDIR

Devi Saraswati, the goddess of knowledge, wisdom, arts, eloquence, and music, is enshrined holding the sacred Veena. Devotees and students seek Her compassionate blessings for intellectual clarity and spiritual awakening.

🕉️ Deity: Devi Saraswati (Goddess of Learning & Arts)
🌸 Key Rituals: Vasant Panchami Mahotsav, Student Vidyarambha Blessings

Shraddha & Saburi Sanctum

श्री साईं बाबा मंदिर

SHRI SAI BABA MANDIR

The sacred sanctum of Shri Sai Baba of Shirdi is set with golden draping, silver pillars, and the eternal teachings of "श्रद्धा" (Unwavering Faith) and "सबूरी" (Patience). Devotees gather for peaceful dhyana and divine blessings.

🕉️ Deity: Shri Sai Baba
🪔 Key Rituals: Thursday Special Prayers, Dhoop Aarti & Bhog Offering

Cosmic Preserver

भगवान विष्णु के दशावतार

VISHNU DASHAVATAR SANCTUM

A grand sacred murti of Bhagwan Vishnu holding the divine Sudarshana Chakra aloft, dressed in resplendent golden pitambar vastra. Honoring the ten divine incarnations who descend to re-establish righteousness (Dharma) across cosmic yugas.

🕉️ Deity: Bhagwan Vishnu (Sudarshana Chakra Swaroop)
📜 Key Rituals: Ekadashi Vrat Pujan, Vishnu Sahasranama Archana

Arogya & Raksha Sanctum

माँ शीतला मंदिर

SHEETLA MATA MANDIR

Revered for granting protection against illnesses, good health, and cooling peace of mind. Families and devotees regularly offer prayers to seek the divine maternal protection of Mata Sheetla Ji.

🕉️ Deity: Mata Sheetla Ji
🪔 Key Rituals: Sheetla Ashtami Pujan & Health Protection Prayers
